# Three-minute demo

**0:00–0:35.** Open the static Overview. Explain the audit-budget problem and show 199,052 public Part B/DME claim groups across eight years. Describe the endpoint as any disagreement among the published rows of a claim group.

**0:35–1:15.** Open Risk models. Compare calibrated lookup and XGBoost on the 2025 holdout. Point out the challenger AP delta and its practical threshold. The lookup remains the active local research scorecard.

**1:15–2:00.** Set Policy replay to 2025, 5% capacity and unweighted. Compare the 10% hybrid's 3.89 percentage point design-aware RMSE with the 16.87 for greedy's selected-only rate. Show the 95.9% greedy yield retained and uniform random's 1.23 percentage point RMSE.

**2:00–2:35.** Open Financial scenarios and move the fictional review cost. Explain that the comparison changes scenario-weighted findings and workload while observed disagreement counts remain grounded in CERT.

**2:35–3:00.** Open Operations, intake a fictional batch and submit a delayed finding. Advance the clock, record a correction and show both receipt versions. In Research extensions, compare adaptive and matched fixed allocation at the same resources.

## Portfolio statements

- Built a local research platform with 199,052 public CMS CERT claim groups and compared calibrated models against a hierarchical baseline on two temporal holdouts.
- Implemented a 200-seed delayed-label replay. At 5% review capacity, a 10% exploration hybrid retained 95.9% of greedy yield and achieved 3.89 percentage points of design-aware RMSE versus 16.87 for greedy’s selected-only rate in the 2025 public cohort.
- Exposed a versioned research scorecard and audit planning through FastAPI and built a static analyst workspace backed by audited aggregate results.
- Built a transactional synthetic review workflow with delayed labels, versioned findings, verifiable decision receipts and a controlled shadow model lifecycle.
