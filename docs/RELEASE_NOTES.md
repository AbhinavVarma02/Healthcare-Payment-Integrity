# Healthcare Payment Integrity v1.0.0 release notes

The first complete release candidate combines the audited CERT study with a local synthetic payment integrity workflow and an aggregate-only static preview.

- Run fictional intake, count-budget allocation, assignment, delayed findings, supervisor corrections and overrides from one workspace. Inspect the original decision, inclusion probability and subsequent review history.
- Reconstruct accepted decisions from stored receipts and compare the event chain against a retained local anchor.
- Fit and validate synthetic model candidates, score them in shadow and require explicit evidence-bound approval for activation or rollback. The audited CERT lookup remains the research reference.
- Explore the retrospective adaptive allocation experiment and synthetic model lifecycle evidence in the policy lab. The static review page illustrates a fictional delayed review and correction.
- Use the deterministic analyst tools for explanations, measured evidence and bounded scenario calculations. The tested local-model mode missed its evaluation gate.

The historical H1–H3 protocol, evidence and aggregate remain reproducible from the audited baseline. Research results cover released CERT claim groups. Synthetic workflows demonstrate the lifecycle under controlled assumptions. External publication and operational payer validation are pending separate authorization and evidence.
