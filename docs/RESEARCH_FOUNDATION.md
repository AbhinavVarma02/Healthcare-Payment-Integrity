# Research foundation

Henderson et al. formulate audit selection as a joint problem of finding high-reward cases and estimating a population quantity. Their IRS experiments motivate evaluating yield and estimation separately. The CERT cohort, response variable and sampling design here are specific to healthcare reimbursement review. There is no direct transfer of tax auditing effect sizes. [AAAI paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25637)

Revelas, Boldea and Werker derive consistency results for investigation-driven selection under assumptions about the conditional outcome model. This work evaluates misspecification and transport in an empirical replay. Inverse-probability weighting can change the target of the fitted loss and its variance. Consistency depends on the selection and outcome assumptions. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18739)

The design-aware estimator uses inverse marginal inclusion probabilities conditional on pre-decision history. Certainty units and independently sampled strata contribute separate terms to the estimate. The sampling frame is the released eligible CERT subset. The original CMS design and estimator concern a different population and outcome. [CMS sampling methodology](https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/CERT%20Methodology.pdf)

Governance mappings use NIST for risk measurement and management, NAIC for insurance-sector documentation context, and banking SR 26-2 for lifecycle and validation practices. These are reference practices for this research prototype. [References](REFERENCES.md)
