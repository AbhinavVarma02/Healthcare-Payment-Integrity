# Research protocol for adaptive review, model updates and analyst tools

The frozen CERT protocol and H1–H3 evidence remain the reference study. H4 uses previously examined cohorts and is retrospective and exploratory. H5 uses generated operational data. H6 evaluates a bounded analyst interface against independent expectations. [Frozen research protocol](research_protocol.md)

## H4 · Adaptive allocation

Use 2022–2025 waves, the frozen replay's feature-only inputs and its initial fitted lookup learner without updates. Evaluate 2%, 5%, 10% and 20% capacity with the first 200 registered replay seeds. Primary comparison is 2025 at 5% capacity. Compare uniform, greedy, fixed 5%, 10% and 20% exploration, adaptive, and a matched fixed schedule. Before each wave, compare the DME share with the previous wave. A change of at least five percentage points uses 20% exploration. Otherwise use 10%. The first wave uses 10%. Report novelty as a diagnostic. For the matched fixed schedule use the prespecified 2022–2023 development rule's mean reserve share, rounded to the nearest whole percent. Also compare realized exploration counts directly across all policies.

Sort ties by the frozen stable key. Reserve at least one exploratory review at partial positive capacity. Use SRS without replacement from the non-targeted tail. Pair policies on a common seed and wave budget. Compare actual findings, yield, review minutes, nonfindings and cohort-rate bias, RMSE, 95% hypergeometric interval coverage, width and estimation ESS. Compute selected-only results for each design as descriptive comparators. Report HT only when the entire frame has positive support. Show paired differences and Monte Carlo standard error, including for coverage and RMSE. The primary practical yield floor is 95% of greedy yield. Assess the yield, RMSE and review-minute Pareto frontier and whether adaptive improves RMSE over matched fixed exploration at that floor. Show other years and capacities as sensitivities. Treat unexecuted or incomplete seeds as missing and withhold complete-grid conclusions. Oracle labels are accessed only in the evaluator. The estimand is the reviewed disagreement indicator averaged over the eligible released cohort. [CERT sampling context](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/improper-payment-measurement-programs/comprehensive-error-rate-testing-cert), [Joint audit selection and estimation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/25637)

## H5 · Controlled learning under delayed feedback

Use a deterministic synthetic generator with 8 waves of 500 independent cases and seeds 20–39. Use same cases and uniform revealed feedback positions across candidate arms. Review 20% of each wave for feedback. Evaluate operational yield at 10% risk-ranked capacity. Test stationary, score-level prevalence increase at wave 4, and subgroup shift at wave 4, each with delays of one and two waves. Development checks use seeds 10–14. Base probabilities use the verified lookup. Generate events by adding 0.9 to log odds for the prevalence shift and 1.8 to log odds for DME-only shift. Train on earlier mature waves, calibrate on the penultimate mature wave and validate on the latest mature wave. Require at least 30 labels per split and both outcomes in calibration and validation. Never train or pick an arm using evaluator-only labels. Use a no-update baseline, recalibration and a refitted hierarchical lookup followed by calibration. Uniform historical feedback within each synthetic wave uses equal weights. Report selection-conditioned limits for any hand-entered operational feedback.

Gates require valid artifact, feature/schema and cutoffs, Brier improvement of at least 0.001, no AP loss greater than 0.025, no fixed-capacity yield loss greater than 0.10, and no absolute subgroup rate error greater than 0.25 for groups with at least ten validation cases. Require finite scores, complete evaluation and measured batch latency under 250 ms for 500 cases on the reference machine. Track bootstrap uncertainty for paired Brier and abstain from promotion when the available validation evidence cannot establish a nonpositive upper 95% bound for Brier loss. Compare mean paired Brier, AP and fixed-capacity yield across post-shift waves, detection delay after the first eligible review signal, first successful recovery and simulated approval/rejection counts. Review burden and inference overhead are diagnostics. Candidate evaluation and approvals refer to the synthetic workspace only. [Model governance context](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)

## H6 · Analyst interface verification

Reserve ten independent scenarios covering exact scenario cents, valid receipt explanation, policy comparisons, retrieval with version and evidence, drift with no data, structured report, missing evidence, invalid arguments and role, an unsupported payment or fraud decision, and a request to retrieve hidden outcomes or execute arbitrary tools. Assert allowlisted typed operation, attribution and numeric equivalence to independently computed fixture values or audited figures. Record wall-time and expected responses before evaluating. Require zero critical unsupported financial claims, unauthorized actions and label disclosures. If a compatible local model is evaluated, run the same suite, compare selection, grounding, absence of fabricated citations, abstention, tool limits and latency. Record the deterministic and generative evidence independently.
