# CERT protocol v1

## Population, information and timing

The analysis unit is one reporting-year control-number group of published Part B or DME rows. The outcome is any recognized Disagree decision, provided every visible row has an Agree or Disagree decision. Unknown outcomes remain unavailable. H1 and H2 exclude them before measuring common eligible outcomes. H3 skips a replay wave containing any unknown outcomes because filtering its selection frame by outcome availability would reveal information. Cohort construction and manifest accounting precede scoring.

The feature schema is part, modal provider category (lexicographic tie break), visible line count and number of visible provider categories. Numerical features follow fixed transformations specified below. Unknown provider strings are encoded as other for ML, with shrinkage backoff for the lookup. Exclude identifiers, billing/diagnosis/procedure codes, decisions, error types and outcome-derived counts from model inputs. A hashed within-wave key is available to make ties deterministic. No monetary or time inputs are inferred from a real claim.

H1 and H2 use retrospective reporting-year splits. Fit 2018–2021, separately calibrate on 2022, select on 2023, and report the final comparison on 2025 with 2024 corroboration. These are temporal generalization checks under public release suppression and vintage limitations. They do not simulate scoring the raw claims at their original dates.

H3 uses a simulated batch decision on each submission window end from 2022 to 2025. Start with 2018–2020 as eligible seed labels. No 2021 labels are used in H3, preserving the common warm start across the four waves. Availability is the catalog version date of the exact source CSV used. The replay therefore waits until 2024 to use any 2022 outcomes and until 2025 to use any 2023 outcomes. The learning interface receives only policy-selected outcomes once available. A separate full-label oracle is an inaccessible comparator. Replay is an experiment on the historically released features and cannot establish how a live prospectively assembled cohort would behave.

## H1 models and decisions

Compare random ranking, global rate, unsmoothed historical part/provider rates with fallback, and a hierarchical empirical-Bayes beta-binomial lookup. Estimate a global method-of-moments prior concentration across provider groups with at least 20 training claims, clamped to [2, 100], by subtracting expected binomial sampling variance. Use 20 if the moment is undefined or sampling variance exhausts observed variance. Center group priors on shrunken part rates. Back off exact provider and visible-line band to provider, and then to part. Line bands are 1, 2–3, 4–6 and 7+. For the provider layer require at least 20 training observations and for a line-band cell require at least 20. The prevalence estimate for the global model uses a Beta(1,1) prior.

ML candidates are one-hot regularized logistic regression (C in [0.1, 1, 10]) and one-hot boosted trees (XGBoost, depths 2 or 4, 120 estimators, learning rate 0.08, minimum child weight 10, single CPU thread, seed 37301). Part and provider string form separate categorical features. Group provider categories with fewer than 20 training observations into other. Use log1p visible lines and provider-type count. Fit transforms on training only. Fit sigmoid calibration by logistic regression on the logit of calibration-set model predictions, with nonpositive calibration slope falling back to the uncalibrated score. Compare to raw candidate metrics. Select the ML candidate with highest development average precision, then lower calibrated Brier, then model ID. Choose the retrospective H2 scoring model by the same rule across the selected ML challenger and lookup. Run a logistic sensitivity omitting both count features and an exploratory development-only recalibration comparison against refitting.

Report average precision (step-wise PR area), AUROC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, fixed decile reliability and ECE, precision/recall at nominal 1/2/5/10% capacity, and Part B/DME strata with at least 100 claims and at least 20 of each outcome. Fixed capacities use round half up to a whole claim. Break ties by ascending within-wave hash. Refit-free paired bootstrap resamples claims with replacement, 400 replicates, fixed seed. Duplicate draws use original positions with stable resample order. Intervals describe empirical claim-level variability and do not estimate nationally weighted or clustered design uncertainty. RY2025 is primary. Require both lower bounds of the two-sided 97.5% individual intervals to exceed their practical thresholds (+0.01 AP, +0.02 precision@5%). If an upper bound is at or below its threshold the joint practical gain is unsupported. Otherwise it is inconclusive. Report 2024 with the same intervals as a secondary comparison.

## H2 financial sensitivity

Comparison uses 1/2/5/10% exact review counts. Primary case is 2025, 5% capacity, higher-nominal DME scenario (Part B 10,000 cents, DME 25,000 cents), hypothetical financially relevant fraction 35%, and review cost 3,000 cents. Secondary grid swaps the Part B and DME values and uses uniform values, fractions 15/35/60%, and review costs 1,500/3,000/6,000 cents. Operational minutes scenarios use 20 minutes base plus 2 minutes per additional visible row, capped at 60 minutes. Compare risk, expected fictional net value and net value per assumed minute, with optional group cap and an exploration reserve where a feasible pool exists. Fixed-capacity comparisons retain all slots even when scenario net is negative. For optional reviews under a minutes limit, skip nonpositive marginal value. All stored scenario amounts are integer cents. Multiply with Decimal and round half up only at the accounting boundary.

Report disagreement yield and weighted findings independently from the predicted objective. Report review share by part, unused capacity and nonfinding reviews per finding, unavailable when there are zero findings. Standard cost and percentage sensitivity use the same observed outcome labels. Primary effect is relative difference in weighted findings at a common capacity and absolute change in nonfinding reviews per finding, with paired percentile bootstrap (400 replicates, two 97.5% intervals). Support requires a lower weighted gain bound above +5% and an upper burden increase below +0.5. A bound wholly on the failing side of either criterion is unsupported. Other outcomes are inconclusive. None of the scenario figures measures actual claim dollars, savings, reimbursement or return on investment.

## H3 replay and estimation

Use uniform, greedy, 5/10/20% exploration and score-stratified SRS. Hybrids reserve a positive integer count for exploration when budget is positive and below the frame size. Pick the top K-r with certainty and sample r without replacement from the remainder. The two-stratum design uses the top score quintile and remaining claims, with half the reviews in the top quintile when feasible and at least one per nonempty stratum. With fewer than two slots use uniform sampling. Use the same capacities across designs. Run 200 deterministic seeds (37301–37500), one sequential worker with checkpointed seed batches.

The replay learner updates hierarchical lookup sufficient statistics after labels become available. Use the warm-start prior concentration for all replay learners. Compare unweighted, full inverse inclusion and weights capped at 20. Each revealed claim adds its supplied weight to counts, without policy-to-policy sharing. Full IPW targets claim-count-weighted simulated waves under positivity. Greedy uses unweighted learning only. Exact HT estimates use realized marginal probabilities. For independent SRS strata use the sum of finite population variance contributions and normal approximations with untrimmed endpoints. A non-census stratum with fewer than two reviews makes variance and intervals unavailable. Greedy can compute a selected-only rate when it lacks positivity. Record bias, RMSE, interval coverage, effective sample size, yield, cumulative findings and future predictive calibration/discrimination. Report Monte Carlo standard errors and quantiles across seeds.

Primary estimation is the absolute 2025 finite-frame bias and RMSE at 5% capacity. The hybrid component is supported if 10% exploration has lower RMSE than the greedy naive estimator by at least 0.01 and mean yield stays within 10% of greedy. Stratified SRS supports an estimation benefit if its RMSE improves on uniform by at least 0.005 with coverage in [0.90, 0.98]. The weighted-learning component supports an improvement if 10% exploration with IPW lowers paired 2025 Brier by at least 0.005 compared with the unweighted 10% learner, with a 95% Monte Carlo interval excluding zero. Treat these methods as distinct component claims and call the complete hypothesis mixed or inconclusive if they disagree or evidence is insufficient. Secondary estimates contextualize practical trade-offs. Conditional HT is unbiased under these designs. This property alone does not establish small variance or successful weighted training.

## Governance and exposure

Model promotion is unavailable for live payer operations. The local registry may activate a research artifact only with an explicit research-only status. A formal promotion candidate requires AP and precision practical gates, holdout Brier no worse than lookup by over 0.005, and no structural, artifact or service failure. No national or individual provider fairness claim can be evaluated from these data.

The supplied project specification disclosed approximate 2024/2025 row counts and prior aggregate 2025 exposure. Official pages also disclose national expenditure-weighted estimates. Prior to the commit containing this protocol we checked only the 2024/2025 structural columns and metadata, without parsing either row-level outcome column or calculating model metrics there. The experiment is an internally frozen historical evaluation with known aggregate context. The local exposure log records authorized evaluation access. A changed source digest, protocol hash or eligibility rule invalidates the frozen run pending documented review.
